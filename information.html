Toronto, Canada

Toronto is the capital city of the Canadian province of Ontario. It is known for its diverse cultural scene, iconic landmarks such as the CN Tower, and vibrant neighborhoods. Toronto also offers a mix of historical and modern attractions, parks, and a beautiful waterfront.

Mecca, Saudi Arabia

Mecca, also known as Makkah, is a city in Saudi Arabia and the holiest city in Islam. Every year, millions of Muslims travel to Mecca to perform the Hajj pilgrimage. The city features significant sites such as the Masjid al-Haram (the Grand Mosque) and the Kaaba.

Bali, Indonesia

Bali is an Indonesian island known for its forested volcanic mountains, iconic rice paddies, beaches, and coral reefs. The island is a renowned tourist destination featuring vibrant nightlife, yoga and meditation retreats, and captivating cultural attractions such as temples and traditional dance performances.
